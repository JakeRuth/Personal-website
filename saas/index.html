Field notes

Five moments that shaped the engineer.

Release notes, not resume bullets. The stories that come up in real conversations.

origin 2010 · senior year

The AP CS switch

Needed a math credit. Refused to take AP Calculus. Ms. Nalbandian mentioned she also taught AP Computer Science. Asked if it counted. She said yes. Took it purely to dodge Calc. Fell in love with code in the first week. Thirteen years later, still doing it.

founding 2020–2021

The Pronk emails

Daniel Pronk spent five to ten hours every weekend on Excel sheets for his investing YouTube content. Wrote a Python automation in a weekend, emailed it to him for free. He ignored me for six weeks. Replied to make me stop. I didn't stop — hopped on a video call with a beer. YC reached out cold via LinkedIn before we'd even applied.

personality Oscar talent show

The unicycle cube

Rode a unicycle while solving a Rubik's cube. Josh Kushner was in the audience. 3×3 competitive average: 13.95 seconds. Competed 2008–2014 at Northeast US events and Nationals. The cube does not care if you are tired.

AI-era 2026

Migrated Stock Unlock off Customer.io onto self-hosted AWS SES with custom admin panels. Built largely with Claude Code, reviewed every line. One person, one weekend of focused work. Saved roughly $6K a year. Driver in the driver's seat, not driven by the car.
