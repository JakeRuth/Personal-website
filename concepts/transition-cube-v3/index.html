Arrived

You're through the cube.

Scrambled, solved, faded out of the way. v3's tuning target was to keep v2's correctness and cut the runtime roughly in half. The destination panel is always here — it just waits its turn.

What v2 already got right

Each cubie is a Group with its stickers as children. Layer rotations use group.attach() / cubeGroup.attach(), which preserve world transforms across reparents. v3 keeps all of it unchanged — that's the load-bearing part.

What v3 tuned

Moves shortened from 220ms to 125ms. Pauses from 90ms to 40ms. Scramble from 8 to 6. Total move time dropped from ~5000ms to ~1800ms. Ambient y-drift at ~0.2 rad/sec keeps the cube constantly in motion.

Fade, not shrink

v2 scaled in from 0.55× and shrunk out to 0.28×. v3 holds a constant size the whole time. Overlay and cube fade in over 200ms, fade out over 250ms. No size pumping.
