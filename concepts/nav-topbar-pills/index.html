What this is

One of thirteen parallel prototypes for the personal-site rebuild. Each prototype is a self-contained experience (XP Luna, Enterprise SaaS, Git Log, README, Vista, …). The persistent top bar is the connective tissue between them — always visible, always one click away from any other mode.

Why a persistent top bar

Each experience is a world of its own. But a visitor who lands on the XP page shouldn't have to backtrack to try the Git Log version. The top bar is the lightest-weight way to offer that: five pills, always reachable, no modals, no menus. Click, cross-fade, you're there.

Filler for scroll

The rest of this page exists so the collapse-on-scroll behavior has something to happen against. The pill bar should compress into a thin dock as soon as you push past the top, then expand back the moment you scroll up.

Real experiences will have real content. Think: a full XP desktop; a full Enterprise SaaS dashboard; a full terminal git log. The top bar is content-agnostic.
