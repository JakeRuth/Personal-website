About this product

Thirteen years shipping. One chapter ending, another beginning. I co-founded Stock Unlock, took it through YC W22, raised $1.335M, and scaled to eight employees and thousands of paying customers. It's profitable. Three people run it day-to-day. I'm not one of them.

Before that: Senior SWE at Oscar Health (2017–2021). Before that: CommerceHub, out of SUNY Albany (3.88 GPA, CS + Applied Math). I took AP Computer Science senior year specifically to dodge AP Calculus. Fell in love with code in the first week. That sideways pivot paid rent ever since.

“Driver in the driver's seat, not driven by the car.” AI writes more code than I do. I read every line before it ships.

What I want

High-bar teams. Brutally honest teammates who disagree directly, with reason. Autonomy and real ownership. Things that matter, that aren't bullshit. Get-shit-done culture.

No compliment-sandwich feedback. No bureaucracy over shipping. No Fisher-Price software that looks polished and breaks under real use. No overcharging people who can't afford to be robbed — that's why Stock Unlock exists, and it's the closest thing I have to a core conviction.

A few true stories

The AP CS switch. Senior year of high school, I needed a math credit and refused AP Calc. Ms. Nalbandian said AP Computer Science counted. I took it to dodge Calc. Fell in love with code in the first week. That's the whole career in one pivot.

The Pronk emails. Daniel Pronk was a YouTuber burning 5–10 hours a weekend on spreadsheets. I wrote the Python to automate it, emailed him free, got ignored. Kept emailing. He answered to make me stop. Instead of going away, I hopped on a video call, cracked a beer, and we started Stock Unlock.

The unicycle cube. Oscar talent show. Rode a unicycle while solving a 3x3. Josh Kushner was in the audience. That's the most Oscar-era sentence I have.
