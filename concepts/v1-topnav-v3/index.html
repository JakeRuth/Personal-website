Three top-nav layouts, side by side.

v2 was cleaner than v1, but too sparse — a small pill on the far left and the tabs cornered on the far right left a gap wider than both elements combined. These three variants each fill that middle differently. Scroll through them. Pick one.

Variant A

Centered tabs

Setup stays anchored far left. The three tabs move to the exact center of the bar. The blank space is evenly split, so the eye doesn't get stranded between two islands. Simplest fix to the v2 gap.

What sits under this bar

The experience below (XP Luna, README, or ruth/systems) starts here. On the real site this card is replaced by the experience chrome. The nav is pinned, the content scrolls underneath.

Jake's quick read: "centered tabs make the empty space feel intentional instead of accidental."

Variant B

Wordmark middle, tabs right

Setup on the left. A serif Jake Ruth wordmark anchors the middle space. Tabs stay grouped as a segmented control on the right. The wordmark is also clickable — a secondary way back to Setup.

The middle becomes a brand moment

Cormorant Garamond, small caps, letter-spaced. Reads as a quiet wordmark — not a logo lockup shouting for attention. Acts like a masthead in a newspaper.

Clicking the wordmark also returns to the installer. Two ways home is fine here — the far-left ← Setup stays primary; the wordmark is an ambient secondary.

About this layout

Pros: most identity. Makes the site feel like a personal site, not a product page. Ties the experiences together under one name.

Cons: the wordmark has to be typeset well or it undercuts the whole bar. On small screens the wordmark collapses, leaving the tabs + Setup split — same failure mode as v2 if not watched.

Variant C

Unified center bar

Everything grouped in one centered pill: [Setup | Old-school OS · Code repo · SaaS product]. The edges of the screen are empty on purpose — the nav is one command-bar unit, Spotlight-shaped.

One control, not two

The problem with v2 wasn't "empty space" as much as "two islands." Variant C stops treating Setup and tabs as separate things and puts them inside the same pill. Divider between them. Dot separators between tabs. Single rounded container around the whole group.

Feels like a macOS command palette. Reads as one affordance with four entry points.

About this layout

Cons: takes a hair more visual real estate in the middle. On very wide screens the empty sides can read as wasted if the content below doesn't also center. Needs consistent page layout to look right.
