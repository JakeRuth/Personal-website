A cube-shaped transition.

The animation you fire between experiences. Scrambled cube fades in large, solves itself with real slice rotations, then shrinks out of the way. Whatever is behind it loads.

Competed 2008–2014. 13.95 second average on a 3x3. The solver is a bidirectional BFS on the cubie state, not a scramble replay — it finds a fresh solution every trigger.

You're through the cube.

The cube finished solving and shrunk out of the way. This panel was always here — it just couldn't fade in until the solve finished. In a real site this is another experience: the terminal, the blueprint, whatever's next on the list.

Why cube-shaped

Personal: I ran track captain and I ran YC, and in between I rode a unicycle while solving a cube on stage. Putting a real solver on the page is not decoration — it's a signature.

Why real

No scramble-then-reverse shortcut. The solver BFSes its way out of whatever state the scramble drops it into. Fresh solution every fire.

Integration

Two scripts, one function call. Works between picker screens, between experiences, or as a full-page navigation cover. See README.md.
