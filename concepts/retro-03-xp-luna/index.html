About Jake — System Properties

I'm a software engineer and founder with about thirteen years of production code under my belt. I built Stock Unlock (YC W22, $1.335M seed) from zero to thousands of paying customers and eight employees at its peak. Today it runs as a profitable side business, and I'm not full-time there.
