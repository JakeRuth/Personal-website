Concept: nav-bottom-segment

Staging · low-chrome navigation

Three doors. One house.

Same content, three takes. The segmented control at the bottom is the whole nav — small enough to ignore, big enough to find. Slide between experiences the way you'd switch tabs on a map app.

Try it

Click a segment. Tab into it. Arrow keys move focus; Enter confirms. Non-current segments trigger the transition cube before landing.

Why a segmented control

A top bar commits to every experience being the same shape. A command palette commits to the visitor knowing what to type. A segmented control commits to almost nothing — three pills, one filled, move between them. That's the cost of the chrome, and it's cheap.

The bar floats 24px off the bottom of the viewport. It stays put while you scroll. No sticky-to-bottom, no disappearing on scroll-down, no clever show/hide. It's just there.
