The cube grows, then shrinks away.

v3 tuned the timing and the solver read. v4 reshapes the choreography: the cube doesn’t just appear and dissolve — it grows from a point, fills the screen while it solves, and then shrinks away as the new page fades in through it.

The opacities are inverses. As the cube fades in, this page fades out on the same curve. As the cube fades out in phase 3, the destination page fades in on the same curve. Source and destination cross at the solved moment.

Through the cube, into the new page.

Phase 3 faded this panel in (opacity 0 → 1) on the same curve that faded the cube out (opacity 1 → 0). That’s the inverse cross-fade from Jake’s brief. In a real page-to-page transition this is the destination URL, hydrated by initArrival() from a sessionStorage handoff.

Phase 1 — grow & leave

Cube scales from ~48px to 100vmax over 700ms. Cube opacity 0 → 1, source page opacity 1 → 0 on the same eased curve.

Phase 2 — peak & solve

Viewport-filling cube plays the real scramble + solve at v3 timing. Ambient ~0.2 rad/sec y-drift stays on throughout. Source at 0, destination still at 0, dark seam backdrop between them.

Phase 3 — shrink & arrive

Cube scales back to ~48px over 700ms. Cube opacity 1 → 0, destination opacity 0 → 1 on the same curve. Inverse of phase 1.
