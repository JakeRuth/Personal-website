What is Ship Jake Ruth™?

Jake Ruth is a software engineer and founder based in New York City. He has been writing code since 2013, co-founded Stock Unlock through Y Combinator (W22), raised a $1.335M seed, and scaled the team to 8 employees and thousands of paying customers. Stock Unlock is profitable and runs as a side business; Jake is no longer full-time there and is deciding what to build next.

This installer wraps thirteen years of production-grade engineering, a working cofounder brain, and an allergy to overcharging for bad software. He's the guy who rode a unicycle solving a Rubik's cube at an Oscar Health talent show. Consider that a reference implementation.
