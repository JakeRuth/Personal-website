Chapter Two

What I bring.

The honest list. Written once, maintained occasionally, and deliberately short.

  1. 01

    Zero‑to‑one product instinct.

    I have taken an idea from a napkin to thousands of paying customers. I have opinions about which napkins to keep.

  2. 02

    A thirteen‑year technical foundation.

    Stock Unlock, Oscar Health, Youni, CommerceHub. Distributed systems, fintech, small products, big products, and the boring parts in between.

  3. 03

    Managing engineers without theatrics.

    Eight at peak. No performance reviews theater, no fake roadmaps. People shipped and they stayed.

  4. 04

    A practical taste in AI.

    Driver‑in‑the‑driver's‑seat, not demo‑driven. LLMs as a tool, not a thesis. Built with them for paying users, not decks.

  5. 05

    A temperament for distance.

    I like the year three of things more than the week two. Side projects that outlive their novelty. A profitable business on the side is still the side.

  6. 06

    A working sense of humor.

    Which has, more than once, been the thing that kept the team together at 3 a.m. on a ship day.
