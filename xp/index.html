AI philosophy — ReadMe.txt

Driver in the driver's seat, not driven by the car. AI writes more code than I do now. I read every line before it ships.

Opus 4.5 was the inflection point. Since then I've migrated Stock Unlock off Customer.io onto self-hosted AWS SES (saved roughly $6K a year), rewritten the marketing site in Webflow with AI-assisted code, and shipped a tightly-permissioned internal Discord bot on EC2 that lets non-technical teammates query logs, errors, code and user data in plain English.

Human-reviewed for business logic, security, and system architecture. AI-only is acceptable for static front-end visualization. There's a real distinction between vibe coding and software engineering.

Stories — Shared Documents

The AP CS switch Senior year of high school, 2010

I needed a math credit senior year. I refused to take AP Calculus. Ms. Nalbandian mentioned she also taught AP Computer Science. I asked if it counted as a math credit. She said yes. I took it purely to dodge Calc. Fell in love with code in the first week. That sideways pivot became my career.

The production takedown CommerceHub, intern era

I took down production once with a single button press. Walked into the meeting nervous and apologized before anyone spoke. My manager stood up and said “that wasn't your fault — you shouldn't have had access to that button.” The system was the problem, not the intern. That's the code-review culture I've been trying to rebuild ever since.

The Pronk emails 2020–2021

Daniel Pronk was spending whole weekends on Excel sheets for his investing YouTube channel. I wrote a Python script in a weekend, emailed it to him free, and kept emailing. He ignored me for about six weeks. He eventually replied to make me stop. I didn't stop — I hopped on a video call with a beer. That's how Stock Unlock got its co-founder.

The unicycle cube Oscar talent show

I rode a unicycle while solving a Rubik's cube. Josh Kushner was in the audience. That's it. That's the whole story. Everything else that day was forgettable.

The Customer.io hack-off 2026

Migrated Stock Unlock off Customer.io onto self-hosted AWS SES with custom admin panels. Built most of it with Claude Code, reviewed every line. One person. One weekend of focused work. Roughly $6K a year saved. This is what the AI-era capability stack actually looks like when you know what you're doing.

CubeMaster XP — A real solver, not a replay

Open CubeMaster XP.exe from the desktop. Scramble the cube, then hit Solve. The solver runs bidirectional breadth-first search on the actual cube state — it handles any reachable position, not just ones we scrambled into. The timer shows how long from scramble start to solved.

Personal best 3×3 average: 13.95 seconds. Competed 2008–2014 at Northeast US and Nationals. Once solved one on a unicycle at an Oscar talent show. Josh Kushner was in the audience.
